\chapter{Getting Help}

When you're in need of help---whether you're trying to figure out how layers work, or if you're
having some other issue with OmniGraffle---it's good to know that the Support Humans at the
Omni Group are always available to lend you a hand.

\section{Release Notes}

For a detailed, comprehensive summary of the latest changes to OmniGraffle, check out
\textbf{Help} $\blacktriangleright$ \textbf{Release Notes} from the menu bar. This is a good
first place to check if something is behaving in a way you didn't expect.

\section{Finding Help Online}

The \href{https://support.omnigroup.com/omnigraffle}{OmniGraffle Support Site} provides a wealth
of information, including Support Articles for answering some of the more technical issues you
might discover, access to OmniGraffle's documentation online, as well as download links for
the latest version of OmniGraffle.

Similarly, the \href{https://www.omnigroup.com/products/omnigraffle}{OmniGraffle product page}
is always a good place to find the latest information about features and updates to
OmniGraffle.

\section{Videos}

In addition to the online help, we also provide a series of
\href{https://www.omnigroup.com/video/omnigraffle/}{online videos} for OmniGraffle. These
videos range in topics from available features in the latest version of OmniGraffle to how-to
tutorial videos to help you level up in your OmniGraffle skills.

\section{Email Support}

If you're stuck, if you have a good idea for the next version of OmniGraffle, or if you just
want to let us know how we're doing, go ahead and send us an email. Choose \textbf{Help}
$\blacktriangleright$ \textbf{Contact Omni} from the menu bar to conjure up a message
addressed to us, or just \href{mailto:omnigraffle@omnigroup.com}{send us an email with your
thoughts}. We take support pretty seriously, so you can expect a prompt response actually
typed by human hands.

\section{Phone Support}

We understand that sometimes you just can't wait for an email response when you're knee-deep in
a project and you're stuck or something isn't working quite right. We get that. When this
happens, you can call our Support Humans at 800-315-OMNI or 206-523-4152. Our Support Humans
are available Monday-Friday, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific Time (-0700 GMT).

\section{Online Forums}

Omni maintains \href{http://discourse.omnigroup.com}{online forums} for all of our products,
which are available to everyone. Come share your questions and ideas with other users, share
OmniGraffle files to show off how you're using OmniGraffle for a particular project, and seek
help from the OmniGraffle community.
